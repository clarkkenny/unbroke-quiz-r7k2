Durabledeep runway, and the invested assets to match

You, you're Quietly Unbothered.

An unexpected expense doesn't wreck your week. It mildly annoys you, and you should know how rare that is. You're past the hardest, most important money you'll ever save, and compounding is quietly doing the work now. Your risk isn't being broke anymore. It's being quietly, expensively stuck while your net worth looks great on paper.

What changes it

  1. Watch for paper wealth. A strong balance sheet trapped in home equity and retirement accounts you can't touch isn't freedom. If you can't spend it without a penalty or selling the roof over your head, it's a number on a screen.
  2. Check you're not house-poor in disguise: the beautiful home that quietly ate your 401(k) contribution and your Friday nights.
  3. Ask the question the whole book builds to. Money is one score. Are your Work, Time, and Life freedoms keeping up, or is the money growing while the life shrinks?
  4. Past a certain point, a few hours a year with a fee-only planner pays for itself. This is the one stage where good advice is worth the fee.

The number says you're winning. The only thing left to confirm is whether you're winning the game you meant to play.
